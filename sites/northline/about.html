How we work

One sponsor. One weekly brief. One scoreboard.

We refuse multi-track programs that dissolve into status meetings. Every engagement has a single executive sponsor, a written weekly brief, and a scoreboard of five numbers. If a number isn't moving, we change the work — not the slide.

Before you write

How long is a typical engagement?

Eight to twelve weeks. We'd rather finish than linger.

Do you work outside the US?

Yes — remotely, with on-site weeks as needed.

Are you a staffing firm?

No. We don't place contractors. We take a problem, then leave.
